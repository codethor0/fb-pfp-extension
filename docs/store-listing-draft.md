**Name:** fb-pfp: Local Profile Photo Rotator

**Short description:** Prepare or change your own Facebook profile picture from photos stored locally in Chrome or Brave.

**Description:**

fb-pfp is a small, free browser extension for organizing local pictures and preparing a Facebook profile-photo change on your own account. Select photos from your device, rotate through them at random or without repeats, and preview the intended photo in Facebook's profile-picture dialog.

Use **Prepare for review** to load a photo without automatically clicking Save. Use **Change now** to request a Save after checking an explicit confirmation box. Daily reminders prepare a review only; they do not automatically publish.

Imported images are resized and re-encoded locally to remove camera metadata, then kept in your browser profile. There is no third-party analytics service, advertising, account subscription, or developer backend. A prepared image may be sent to Facebook before you click Save.

Requires a Facebook account that you own. The extension depends on Facebook's English-language interface and may stop working when its interface changes. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc.
