# Privacy policy

## Data handled

This extension processes only images you explicitly import into it. The browser converts them to smaller JPEG images and stores those images in the extension's local IndexedDB. Each record also includes the original image filename, sanitized for display, and simple dimensions and timestamps. Original files remain in your chosen folder.

The browser stores rotation mode, the optional local schedule, IDs of images already acknowledged by Save, and a short last-operation status. The extension does not request or store Facebook passwords, cookies, authentication tokens, or full browsing history.

## Sharing and transmission

There are no developer servers, analytics, advertising endpoints, cloud storage services or external telemetry calls. A manually prepared picture or a scheduled review hands one converted JPEG to an upload input on `https://www.facebook.com`. **Facebook may receive that picture at upload-input time even before you press Save.** Selecting Change now explicitly requests a Save inside the profile-picture dialog. The extension author does not receive the picture.

## Retention and control

Images are held in the current browser profile. Delete individual imported pictures using the popup; disable scheduling at any time. Uninstalling the extension ordinarily removes its local data, though browser backups or synced profiles may retain independent copies. Storage is not shared between Chrome and Brave or different extension IDs.

## Status

Version 0.2.1, experimental. This policy describes the included source behavior. Any future store listing must supply the current public policy and meet the applicable browser-store requirements.
